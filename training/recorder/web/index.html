Tips for useful recordings

Start with your normal voice in a quiet room, about 40–60 cm from your Mac. Do not stretch the word or use an artificially high pitch.

Follow the cues: calmer, softer, or a little farther away. Record one phrase per take, usually 2–4 seconds. Recording stops automatically after 12 seconds.

Take a break and change position slightly before sessions two and three. Final test recordings stay separate from training.

The Mac and Echo have different microphones. After training, test the model with live speech on Echo. The short target “Мышка” also occurs within “Привет, Мышка”.
